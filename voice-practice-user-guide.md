# Voice Practice User Guide

## 🎤 Welcome to Voice Practice!

Voice Practice is an interactive way to improve your social skills by having conversations with an AI coach. The AI will listen to what you say and help you practice different social situations.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Allow Microphone Access
1. **Click the microphone button** when you first start Voice Practice
2. **Allow microphone access** when your browser asks for permission
3. **Speak clearly** into your microphone

### Step 2: Start Your First Conversation
1. **Click the microphone button** to start listening
2. **Say something** like "Hi, I want to practice social skills"
3. **Wait for the AI** to respond with a scenario
4. **Practice your response** and continue the conversation

## 🎯 How Voice Practice Works

### The AI Coach
- **Listens to you speak** using speech recognition
- **Responds with scenarios** like "You're at lunch and want to talk to someone new"
- **Gives you feedback** on your responses
- **Helps you practice** different social situations

### Conversation Flow
1. **You speak** → AI listens and understands
2. **AI responds** → Gives you a scenario or feedback
3. **You practice** → Try different responses
4. **AI coaches** → Helps you improve

## 🎤 Microphone Setup

### Browser Permissions
- **Chrome/Edge**: Click the microphone icon in the address bar
- **Firefox**: Click the shield icon and allow microphone access
- **Safari**: Go to Safari > Preferences > Websites > Microphone

### Tips for Clear Speech
- **Speak clearly** and at a normal volume
- **Avoid background noise** like music or TV
- **Wait for the microphone** to turn on (it will show a green light)
- **Speak directly** into your microphone

### Troubleshooting Microphone Issues
- **Check permissions**: Make sure your browser can use your microphone
- **Test your microphone**: Try recording yourself in another app
- **Check volume**: Make sure your microphone isn't muted
- **Try a different browser**: Some browsers work better than others

## 🤖 What to Expect from the AI Coach

### The AI Will:
- **Give you scenarios** to practice
- **Listen to your responses** and understand them
- **Provide helpful feedback** and suggestions
- **Encourage you** when you do well
- **Help you try again** if something doesn't work

### The AI Won't:
- **Judge you** or make you feel bad
- **Remember your conversations** after you're done
- **Share your practice** with anyone else
- **Replace real human interaction**

## 📱 Using Voice Practice

### Starting a Session
1. **Go to the Voice Practice** section in the app
2. **Click the microphone button** to start
3. **Wait for the green light** to show it's listening
4. **Start speaking** when you're ready

### During a Session
- **Speak naturally** - don't worry about being perfect
- **Try different responses** - the AI will help you explore options
- **Ask questions** if you don't understand something
- **Take breaks** if you need to

### Ending a Session
- **Click the microphone button** to stop listening
- **Say "goodbye"** or "I'm done" to end the conversation
- **Your progress** will be saved automatically

## 🎯 Practice Scenarios

### Common Scenarios You'll Practice:
- **Starting conversations** with new people
- **Joining group activities** at school
- **Asking for help** when you need it
- **Handling disagreements** with friends
- **Making new friends** in different situations

### Example Practice Session:
1. **AI**: "You're at lunch and see someone sitting alone. What would you say?"
2. **You**: "Hi, can I sit here?"
3. **AI**: "Good start! That's a friendly way to approach someone. What if they say yes?"
4. **You**: "Thanks! How's your day going?"
5. **AI**: "Excellent! You're showing interest in them. Keep practicing!"

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "I can't hear the AI"
- **Check your volume** - make sure it's turned up
- **Try refreshing** the page
- **Check if other sounds** work on your device

### "The AI doesn't understand me"
- **Speak more clearly** and slowly
- **Check your microphone** - make sure it's working
- **Try saying it differently** - use simpler words

### "The microphone won't turn on"
- **Check browser permissions** - allow microphone access
- **Try a different browser** - Chrome usually works best
- **Restart your device** if nothing else works

### "The AI keeps saying the same thing"
- **Try saying something different** - the AI responds to what you say
- **Ask a question** to change the direction
- **Say "let's try something else"** to get a new scenario

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is my voice being recorded?
**A:** No, your voice is only processed to understand what you're saying, but it's not saved or recorded anywhere.

### Q: Can other people hear my practice?
**A:** No, your practice sessions are private. Only you can hear what you're saying.

### Q: What if I make a mistake?
**A:** That's okay! Making mistakes is part of learning. The AI will help you try again.

### Q: How long should I practice?
**A:** Start with 5-10 minutes. You can practice as long as you want, but take breaks when you need them.

### Q: Will this help me in real life?
**A:** Yes! Practicing with the AI helps you feel more confident when talking to real people.

### Q: What if I don't know what to say?
**A:** The AI will give you suggestions and help you think of responses. Just try your best!

## 👶 Age-Appropriate Instructions

### For Kindergarten - 2nd Grade (Ages 5-7)
- **Keep it simple**: Use short, easy words
- **Practice basic greetings**: "Hi", "Hello", "How are you?"
- **Take turns**: Listen when the AI talks, then you talk
- **Have fun**: This is like playing a game with a friendly robot

### For 3rd - 5th Grade (Ages 8-10)
- **Practice school situations**: Lunch, recess, group work
- **Try different responses**: See what works best
- **Ask questions**: "What should I say if...?"
- **Build confidence**: The more you practice, the easier it gets

### For 6th - 8th Grade (Ages 11-13)
- **Handle peer pressure**: Practice saying no politely
- **Make new friends**: Practice starting conversations
- **Handle conflicts**: Learn to disagree respectfully
- **Build social skills**: Practice for real-life situations

### For 9th - 12th Grade (Ages 14-18)
- **Practice job interviews**: Answer questions confidently
- **Handle social media**: Practice online communication
- **Build relationships**: Practice deeper conversations
- **Prepare for adulthood**: Practice professional communication
